01 // INTRODUCTION TO THE NOC ACTIVE
THE 24/7 COMMAND CENTER

THE NOC:
HEARTBEAT OF OUR NETWORK

> WHAT IS THE NOC?
Think of it as the ultimate control room for the entire network that never sleeps—operating 24/7.

> WHAT DOES IT DO?
It keeps a constant eye on everything happening across the network to catch problems instantly, often fixing them before customers even notice.

> WHY IS IT IMPORTANT?
Without it, we wouldn't have a centralized way to track outages, manage traffic, and keep millions of people connected around the clock.
