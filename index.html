01 — NSOC Overview

Network & Service Operations Center

The centralized nerve center of Omantel's telecommunications infrastructure, orchestrating nationwide digital resilience 24/7/365.

  • Continuous surveillance of physical network elements and digital services.
  • Holistic operational cycle spanning event detection, incident resolution, and preventive tuning.
  • Unifying national connectivity under a single command umbrella.
02 — Hierarchy & Escalation

Front Office & Back Office Structure

A synchronized multi-tier operational model engineered for rapid isolation, expert analysis, and seamless field execution.

Front Office (Tier 1)

Live alarm surveillance, initial triage, and customer impact tracking.

Back Office (Tier 2/3)

Domain engineers driving root-cause diagnosis and complex remediation.

Escalation Workflow

Front Office → Back Office → Field Intervention / Vendor Partner

03 — Organizational Structure

Core Operational Domains

Specialized technical verticals working in unison to protect every architectural layer of Omantel's network.

  • Fixed & Service Desk: Broadband operations and subscriber request management.
  • Transport & Transmission: Optical fiber, microwave links, and VSAT systems.
  • IP Core & Datacom: High-capacity routing, switching, and enterprise data pathways.
  • INOC & Enterprise: Submarine international gateways, MPLS, ILL, and SD-WAN.
  • Service Operations & Cybersecurity: Availability monitoring, threat detection, and response.
04 — Fixed & Service Desk

Fixed Networks & Subscriber Support

Managing fixed-line integrity and governing end-to-end customer service lifecycles.

Fixed Operations

Real-time alarm monitoring, threshold metrics, and automated ticketing.

Service Desk

Service request handling, incident logging, and strict SLA/KPI enforcement.

05 — Transport & Datacom

Transport Links & Datacom Infrastructure

Securing physical backbone connectivity and high-speed data transmission across the nation.

  • IP Core: IP network routing, core reachability, and high-performance packet services.
  • Transmission: Fiber optic lines, microwave relays, VSAT, and optical transport links.
  • Datacom: Enterprise data connectivity, infrastructure monitoring, and troubleshooting.

Enterprise Operations
