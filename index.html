01 — NSOC Overview

Network & Service Operations Center

The core heartbeat of Omantel's telecommunications architecture, unifying network surveillance and service delivery into a single cohesive ecosystem.

  • 24/7/365 continuous national monitoring and operational command.
  • Simultaneous oversight of underlying network infrastructure and customer-facing services.
  • Complete operational cycle: Event Management, Incident Management, Problem Management, Change Management, and Preventive Maintenance.
02 — Hierarchy & Escalation

Front Office & Back Office Structure

A highly structured multi-tier operational model engineered for rapid isolation and flawless technical resolution.

Front Office

First-line defense managing incoming alarms, live event tracking, and initial triage.

Back Office

Specialized domain engineers executing deep root-cause analysis and complex mitigation.

Escalation Pathway

Front Office → Back Office → Field Intervention / Vendor Partner

03 — Organizational Structure

Core Operational Domains

Specialized technical verticals working in unison to keep every layer of the network performing at elite standards.

  • Fixed & Service Desk: Fixed-line voice/broadband and subscriber request routing.
  • Transport & Transmission: Optical fiber, microwave backhaul, and VSAT links.
  • IP Core & Datacom: Packet routing, enterprise connectivity, and switching infrastructure.
  • INOC & Enterprise: International submarine gateways, MPLS, ILL, and SD-WAN.
  • Service Operations & Cybersecurity: Service availability, degradation tracking, threat detection, and mitigation.
04 — Fixed & Service Desk

Fixed Networks & Subscriber Support

Managing fixed-line integrity, voice channels, and comprehensive customer-facing request lifecycles.

Fixed Operations

Real-time alarm monitoring, threshold tracking, and automated ticketing for fixed broadband and voice.

Service Desk

Handling service requests, incident logging, ticket management, and strict SLA/KPI enforcement.

05 — Transport & Datacom

Transport Links & Datacom Infrastructure

Securing end-to-end physical connectivity and high-speed data transmission across national networks.

  • IP Core: High-capacity IP network routing, core services, and backbone reachability.
  • Transmission: Optical fiber links, microwave relays, VSAT, and optical transport monitoring.
  • Datacom: Enterprise-grade data connectivity, network infrastructure troubleshooting, and alarm isolation.
06 — INOC & Enterprise Services

International Gateways & Corporate Solutions

Connecting Oman to global submarine cable systems and delivering enterprise-class network architectures.

INOC (International NOC)

Overseeing submarine cables, international Internet gateways, and cross-border voice/IP transit.

Enterprise Operations

Dedicated management for MPLS, Point-to-Point (P2P), Leased Lines (ILL), and SD-WAN.

07 — Service Operations & Security

Service Integrity & Cyber Protection

Guaranteeing optimal service performance while defending critical infrastructure against digital threats.

  • Service Operations: Tracking service availability, measuring performance degradation, and coordinating incident impact analysis.
  • Cybersecurity: Continuous security monitoring, threat detection, incident response, and active coordination with network operations.
08 — Operations & Alarms

Alarm Management & Monitoring Systems

The technological backbone driving automated event correlation and network visibility.

Alarm Lifecycle

Generation → Collection → Display → Correlation → Severity Filtering → Acknowledgement → Escalation → Clearance.

Operational Systems

Network Management Systems (NMS), Ticketing platforms, topology mapping, and knowledge documentation repositories.

09 — Incident Workflow

Ticket Lifecycle & Department Interaction

A standardized protocol ensuring seamless transitions from initial detection to final verification.

  • Lifecycle Steps: Detection → Analysis → Classification → Assignment → Escalation → Investigation → Resolution → Verification → Closure.
  • Cross-Department Interaction: Alarm Event → Front Office Triage → Relevant Domain Back Office → Field/Vendor Action → Verification & Closure.
10 — Escalation & KPIs

Escalation Levels & Performance Metrics

Measuring operational excellence through defined escalation tiers and stringent performance indicators.

Escalation Hierarchy

Level 1 (Front Office) → Level 2 (Back Office) → Level 3 (Domain Experts) → Level 4 (Vendor / Management).

Key Performance Indicators

Availability, MTTA, MTTR, alarm volume, critical incident rate, and SLA compliance.

11 — Strategy & Simulation

Proactive Operations & Incident Simulation

Shifting from reactive recovery to predictive prevention across a unified ecosystem.

  • Reactive: Alarm triggered → Incident logged → Troubleshooting → Recovery.
  • Proactive: Continuous monitoring → Advanced analysis → Prediction → Preventive maintenance.
  • Complete Simulation: Transport link failure → Automated alarm → Front Office detection → Back Office investigation → Field/Vendor fix → Service restoration & closure.
