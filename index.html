01 // INTRODUCTION TO THE NOC ACTIVE
THE 24/7 COMMAND CENTER

THE NOC:
HEARTBEAT OF OUR NETWORK

> WHAT IS THE NOC?
Think of it as the ultimate control room for the entire network that never sleeps—operating 24/7.

> WHAT DOES IT DO?
It keeps a constant eye on everything happening across the network to catch problems instantly, often fixing them before customers even notice.

> WHY IS IT IMPORTANT?
Without it, we wouldn't have a centralized way to track outages, manage traffic, and keep millions of people connected around the clock.

02 // TEAMS & DOMAINS ACTIVE
OUR OPERATIONAL STRUCTURE // STAGE_02

INSIDE THE NOC:
TEAMS & NETWORK DOMAINS

> TEAMS & SUPPORT

• Front Office: The 24/7 first line of defense watching alarms and taking initial actions.

• Back Office: Engineering specialists, shift leads, managers, and quality teams handling deep fixes.

• Service Desk: The primary point of contact managing tickets and requests.

• iNOC: Manages international gateways and cross-border traffic.

> CORE NETWORK DOMAINS (ROADMAP)

• Transport: Transmission & IP

• Mobile: Core, SOC, & RAN

• Fixed Access: Fiber & Broadband

• Datacom: MPLS and data connectivity.

03 // INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION ACTIVE
OPERATIONS PROTOCOL // STAGE_03

INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION:
SEVERITY LEVELS

> P1 — Emergency: Critical outage / massive impact

> P2 — Critical: Severe service degradation

> P3 — Major: Significant issue / immediate action

> P4 — Minor: No traffic or service impact

> P5 — Warning: Early indicator / threshold alert

04 // INCIDENT MANAGEMENT PROCESS ACTIVE
OPERATIONS WORKFLOW // STAGE_04

INCIDENT MANAGEMENT
PROCESS FLOW

1. Detect: Alarm via AgileOps / NMS

2. Identify: Responsible Team / Service / Company

3. Ticket & Work Order: Automatically generated when applicable

4. Escalate if Required: Back Office • Higher Level • External Party • FM

5. Troubleshoot & Resolve: Technical investigation

6. Verify & Close: Service restored → Ticket closed

FM Rule: FM remains on-site until site alarms are cleared.

06 // MOBILE NETWORK: RAN ACTIVE
RADIO ACCESS NETWORK // STAGE_06

RAN:
RADIO ACCESS NETWORK

> Overview: Connects user devices to the mobile core network through the radio access network.

> Network Architecture:

• User Device: Smartphone / Mobile Device

• Radio Network: BTS / NodeB / eNodeB / gNodeB + Baseband

• Core Network: MSC • VLR • HLR • etc.

• External Networks: PSTN • PLMN • Internet • Other Networks

> Mobile Network Evolution: 1G (AMPS) → 2G (GSM) → 3G (UMTS / HSPA) → 4G (LTE) → 5G (NR)

* Note: 3G has been phased out.

Tools: Ericsson E&M • Huawei U2020

07 // MOBILE CORE & SOC ACTIVE
CORE & OPERATIONS // STAGE_07

MOBILE CORE & SOC

SOC: Monitors network security, traffic, and performance.

• Security & Firewall Monitoring • Network Performance & KPIs • Alarm & Fault Management

Core: Monitors the mobile core and signaling to maintain service continuity.

• PS • CS • IMS • NFVi • Signaling & Node Monitoring • Traffic, KPIs & KQIs • Alarm & Fault Management

Operations (Analyze → Troubleshoot → Escalate → Follow Up → Resolve):

• Calls & Email Handling • Tickets & Work Orders • Change Requests • Shift Handover

Key Systems: SmartCare • NetCool • U2020 • DRA • STP • Avaya • AgileOps

Flow: RAN → Core → Services / External Networks

08 // FIXED ACCESS: FIBER & BROADBAND ACTIVE
FIXED INFRASTRUCTURE // STAGE_08

FIXED ACCESS:
FIBER & BROADBAND

> Overview: Connects customers to fixed broadband and voice services through the access network.

> Access Technologies & Devices:

• GPON: Fiber-based access technology | OLT: Network-side optical equipment

• ONT: Customer-side optical equipment | MSAN: Provides multiple fixed access services

• NGN: Next-gen voice/network | PRI: Digital voice | MPLS: Private data

> Network Flow: OLT → Fiber Distribution → ONT → Customer

> Monitoring: Fiber/GPON/OLT Alarms • NGN & IGW • Core U2020 • Critical Exchanges • ITOC • Public Wi-Fi • CCTV

Key Systems: Fixed NCE • U2020 • AgileOps • Avaya

09 // DATACOM & iNOC ACTIVE
DATA & INTERNATIONAL CONNECTIVITY // STAGE_09

DATACOM & iNOC

Datacom: Provides data connectivity between sites, customers, and network services.

• ILL: Internet Leased Line (Dedicated) • P2P: Point-to-Point • SD-WAN: Software-Defined WAN (MPLS + Internet, Flexible, Optimized)

iNOC: Monitors and manages international connectivity and traffic.

• Monitoring: Network Weather Map • iNOC Console • IGW Network • Zoi (GGC)

• International Network: IGW Providers & Customers • Zain • GGC • MPLS Core • IGW Peers • Content Providers • STCs • IX Peers • CDNs

Focus: Datacom → Data Connectivity | iNOC → International Connectivity

10 // SERVICE DESK COMPLETE
FOCAL POINT & CONCLUSION // STAGE_10

SERVICE DESK

> Role: The focal point of the NOC, coordinating activities across all NOC teams.

• Monitors Work Orders & CRs • Manages Tickets & WOs • Assigns FM teams • Follows up on SLA & escalation • Handles customer & internal complaints

> CR Levels: Low (Not service affecting) • Medium (Service affecting) • High (Service affecting) • Emergency (Critical / urgent)

> Systems: AgileOps (Auto) • Excel • QMS

> Flow: Complaint/Alarm/CR → Ticket/WO → FM/Team → Follow-Up & Escalation → Resolution

Service Desk = The Focal Point Connecting NOC Teams, FM & Customers
